How we check a deal

Price history

Where trackers have recorded past prices, we mark a deal as a record low, near-record low, a price it often hits, or one that was cheaper recently.

Other retailers

When several stores sell the same product, we compare them. A deal more than 2% above the cheapest is marked "Lower elsewhere".

The was-price

If one store's was-price is more than 10% above another's for the same product, we measure the discount from the lower one. That's the "real" discount.

The score

Each deal gets a score out of 100: mostly from the verdict, then up to 28 points for how deep the real discount is.

"Not yet verified" means the maths is right against the quoted was-price, but we haven't found price history or a second retailer to confirm it. It's not a judgment on the retailer. Very deep discounts with no history, and discounts on a store's own brand, are marked "Was-price unconfirmed" for the same reason. Prices come from retailer pages and dated deal reports, change through the day, and may be member-only or colour-specific. Always confirm the price at checkout. Strikethrough is independent and isn't paid by the retailers listed.
