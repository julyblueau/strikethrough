Retailers and brands: corrections
If you think a price, a was-price or a verdict about one of your products is wrong, email hello@strikethrough.com.au with the product, the page or link on our site, and what's incorrect (a screenshot or a link to the current price helps). We aim to review corrections within two business days, and we'll update or remove anything we can't support. Corrections are free. We don't accept payment to change a verdict.
