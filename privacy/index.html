Privacy policy

Last updated 29 November 2026

Strikethrough ("we", "us") is an independent deal-checking site run by Cameron Gibbens in Melbourne, Australia. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Questions go to hello@strikethrough.com.au.

What we collect

  • Email sign-ups. If you join a list or register interest in Strikethrough Pro, we keep your email address and anything else you choose to give in that form (such as your name, company or whether you're a shopper or a retailer), plus the date and the country your connection came from.
  • Price-drop alerts. If you ask us to tell you when a product gets cheaper, we keep your email address, the product, the price at the time and when we last emailed you about it.
  • "Check a deal" requests. What you type or paste into the Check a deal box is matched against our list in your browser and isn't sent to us, with two exceptions. If you ask us to check a product we don't track, we keep what you entered, any price, and your email if you give one. If you paste a shortened link (for example from the Google app or an Amazon short link), we send that link to our server to find the page it points to. We don't keep it.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics (through Google Tag Manager), which uses cookies and similar technology to record how the site is used: pages viewed, the device and browser, approximate location, how you arrived, and actions such as clicking through to a retailer, running a check or signing up. We use this to see what's useful and improve the site. We don't use Google's advertising features with it.
  • Stored on your device. The site saves a few things in your own browser only, such as the deals you've added to compare, whether you've closed a banner, and your email so you don't have to retype it. You can clear these in your browser settings.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

How we use it

To send you what you asked for (alerts, list emails, a reply to your check request), to run and improve the site, and to keep it secure. We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it with retailers.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers to run the site:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and stores form submissions.
  • Google provides analytics.
  • An email delivery service (Resend) sends price-drop alerts once alerts are switched on.

These providers may store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States. We only give them what they need to provide their service.

Affiliate links

Some links to retailers are affiliate links. When you click one, that retailer (for example Amazon) may set its own cookies to record the referral. That's handled under the retailer's own privacy policy, not this one. Affiliate commissions never change a verdict or a score.

Emails and unsubscribing

We only email you about what you signed up for. Every alert and list email has an unsubscribe link, and we act on unsubscribe requests straight away (and always within five working days). You can also email hello@strikethrough.com.au.

How long we keep it

We keep list sign-ups and alerts until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. We keep check requests for up to 12 months.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@strikethrough.com.au.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Security

We keep only what we need. Form submissions are stored with our hosting provider, and only Strikethrough can access the export. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, email hello@strikethrough.com.au and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

We'll update this page if what we collect or how we use it changes, and change the date at the top.
